GP Digital Twin - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. About the Application

GP Digital Twin is a university graduation project proof of concept developed by students at Cairo University. The project explores the use of physiological and activity data to support the development and evaluation of a digital twin for sports and health-related applications.

2. Data We Access

With the user's authorization, the application may access data provided through the WHOOP API, including recovery, sleep, cycle, workout, profile, and body measurement information.

3. Purpose of Data Use

During the current proof-of-concept phase, WHOOP data is used to understand the structure, availability, and characteristics of the data provided by the WHOOP API and to evaluate its suitability for the project's research objectives.

The current proof-of-concept does not sell WHOOP data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Data Storage

Any WHOOP data collected during testing is handled only for the purposes of the project and is limited to authorized project development and evaluation activities.

5. User Control

Users may revoke the application's access to their WHOOP account through their WHOOP account settings or by otherwise disabling the integration.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and collected data from unauthorized access. WHOOP access credentials and tokens are not intended to be publicly exposed.
